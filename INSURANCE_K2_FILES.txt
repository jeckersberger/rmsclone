DATABASE TABLES CREATED:
=========================

1. insurance_policies
   - Stores insurance policy information
   - Columns: id, instances_id, name, provider, policy_number, coverage_type
   - Coverage types: all_risk, transport, liability, equipment, event
   - Fields: coverage_amount, deductible, premium_monthly, valid_from, valid_until
   - Optional: document_path, notes
   - Boolean: is_active
   - Timestamps: created_at, updated_at

2. insurance_asset_coverage
   - Maps policies to assets/asset_types/stock_items
   - Flexible coverage assignment (single asset or entire type)
   - Columns: id, policy_id, asset_id, asset_type_id, stock_item_id
   - Timestamp: created_at

3. insurance_client_certificates
   - Tracks client insurance certificates
   - Types: liability, property, event
   - Columns: id, instances_id, client_id, certificate_type, file_path
   - Tracking: valid_until, verified, verified_by, verified_at
   - Timestamp: created_at

4. insurance_claims
   - Tracks insurance claims
   - Status values: draft, submitted, under_review, approved, partially_approved, 
                   rejected, paid, closed
   - Columns: id, instances_id, policy_id, damage_workflow_id (optional)
   - Tracking: claim_number, claim_date, description, claimed_amount, approved_amount
   - Optional: payout_date
   - Timestamps: created_at, updated_at
